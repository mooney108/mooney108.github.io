Fast facts

What makes sharks extraordinary

🧠

Extraordinary senses

Sharks can detect electrical fields, vibrations, and even faint traces of blood in the water.

🌊

Ocean balance

By keeping prey populations in check, sharks help maintain healthy marine ecosystems.

🔄

Ancient lineage

Sharks have evolved over millions of years, surviving mass extinctions and adapting to changing seas.

Why they matter

Sharks are a vital part of the ocean food web

When shark populations decline, the balance of marine life can shift. From reef fish to oceanic predators, sharks influence who eats what and help protect biodiversity in the sea.

  • Support healthy reef ecosystems
  • Keep prey species in balance
  • Help protect coral and coastal habitats
